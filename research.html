Alessandra R. Brazzale - Research

Research

Current research interests

My research interests focus on statistical theory and methodology, with particular emphasis on higher-order asymptotics, likelihood and likelihood-type inference, and inference in nonregular statistical problems. I am also interested in measurement error models, model-based classification and clustering, statistical computing, and the development of statistical software.

My methodological research is complemented by interdisciplinary applications in a variety of scientific fields, including astrostatistics, cosmology, particle physics, life sciences, and pharmacovigilance.
